About BAOMI WANG

I am a software engineer, AI builder, and independent researcher based in Hangzhou, China.

I founded DeepMem Labs LLC to explore practical memory infrastructure for AI agents and long-running intelligent systems. I am also Co-founder and CTO of Guiyi Intelligent, where I work on large language model applications.

My earlier work covered network security at Sangfor Technologies, public cloud at Huawei, and big data platforms at Shulan Technology.
